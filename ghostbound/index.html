How to play

Roll 1–5 to move. Roll 👻 and you stay put while the Ghost moves 5 tiles toward whoever is in the lead — and pounces if they are within reach. Anyone on the tile where it stops is caught too.

Caught = back 5 tiles. Caught 3 times = eliminated. Land exactly on 100 to escape.

🕸️ Web: skip a turn · 🐍 Snake: slide to its tail · 🃏 Mystery: draw a card · 🌀 Portal: random teleport · 🛡️ Safe Zone: the Ghost can't touch you.

Landing on another player bumps them back 2 tiles.
